Just wrapped up an incredible sprint designing the new analytics dashboard! 🚀 One of the biggest challenges was taking complex data and making it feel approachable. We leaned heavily into minimalist principles and a glassmorphic aesthetic to keep the interface light and breathable. Key takeaways: 1️⃣ Whitespace is your best friend when dealing with dense data. 2️⃣ Subtle depth > heavy shadows. 3️⃣ Always prioritize user intent over visual flair.
